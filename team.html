Team

 

 

Front Left are a trio of music lovers who have bonded over a shared passion for the dance floor.Coming from different backgrounds, such as Hong Kong, Ireland and England, each member brings aunique vibe and musical identity. They are now based in Verbier, Switzerland, where Front Left wasborn to provide quality sound design and great music programming while installing their own lights and decoration to transform any space in their own vision. They have grown from one or two events during the winter season to sell out events all year and bookings across europe.
